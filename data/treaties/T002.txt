QUOTA SHARE REINSURANCE AGREEMENT
Bluefield Casualty QS 2026 | Treaty Reference: T002

Article 1: Parties
This Agreement is made between Bluefield Insurance (the "Company", ceding company code 4475) and the Reinsurers subscribing hereto.

Article 2: Period
This Agreement shall apply to policies incepting on or after 1 January 2026 and on or before 31 December 2026.

Article 3: Business Covered
General liability and workers' compensation business written by the Company.

Article 4: Reinsurer's Share
The Reinsurers shall accept a 25% quota share of all business ceded under this Agreement.

Article 5: Premium and Commission
The Reinsurers shall allow a ceding commission of 27.5%.

Endorsement No. 1 (effective 1 August 2026)
Article 5 is amended: the ceding commission is increased from 27.5% to 29%. All other terms remain unchanged.
