QUOTA SHARE REINSURANCE AGREEMENT
Harbor Point Marine QS 2026 | Treaty Reference: T003

Article 1: Parties
This Agreement is made between Harbor Point Assurance (the "Company", ceding company code 6120) and the Reinsurers subscribing hereto.

Article 2: Period
This Agreement shall apply to policies incepting on or after 1 January 2026 and on or before 31 December 2026.

Article 3: Business Covered
Marine cargo and hull business written by the Company.

Article 4: Reinsurer's Share
The Reinsurers shall accept a 20% quota share of all business ceded under this Agreement.

Article 5: Premium and Commission
The Reinsurers shall allow a ceding commission of 25%.

Endorsement No. 1 (effective 1 July 2026)
Article 4 is amended: for policies incepting on or after 1 July 2026, the Reinsurers' share is reduced from 20% to 15%. All other terms remain unchanged.
