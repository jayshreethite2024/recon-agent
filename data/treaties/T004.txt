QUOTA SHARE REINSURANCE AGREEMENT
Crestline Motor QS 2026 | Treaty Reference: T004

Article 1: Parties
This Agreement is made between Crestline General (the "Company", ceding company code 5583) and the Reinsurers subscribing hereto.

Article 2: Period
This Agreement shall apply to policies incepting on or after 1 April 2026 and on or before 31 March 2027.

Article 3: Business Covered
Private and commercial motor business written by the Company.

Article 4: Reinsurer's Share
The Reinsurers shall accept a 30% quota share of all business ceded under this Agreement.

Article 5: Premium and Commission
The Reinsurers shall allow a ceding commission of 22%.
