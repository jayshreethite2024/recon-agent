QUOTA SHARE REINSURANCE AGREEMENT
Northwind Property QS 2026 | Treaty Reference: T001

Article 1: Parties
This Agreement is made between Northwind Mutual (the "Company", ceding company code 7310) and the Reinsurers subscribing hereto.

Article 2: Period
This Agreement shall apply to policies incepting on or after 1 January 2026 and on or before 31 December 2026.

Article 3: Business Covered
Property business written by the Company, including residential and commercial fire and allied perils.

Article 4: Reinsurer's Share
The Reinsurers shall accept a 20% quota share of all business ceded under this Agreement.

Article 5: Premium and Commission
The Company shall cede premium in proportion to the Reinsurers' share. The Reinsurers shall allow a ceding commission of 30%.

Article 6: Accounts
Accounts shall be rendered quarterly. Amounts posted after the quarter cut-off shall be included in the following cycle's account.
